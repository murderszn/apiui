What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better browsing experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our site.

Key Points About Cookies

Small Text Files

Stored on your device when visiting websites

Improve Experience

Remember preferences and usage patterns

Secure Storage

Cannot execute code or contain viruses

Controllable

You can manage and delete cookies anytime

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several purposes to enhance your experience on apiUI:

Essential Cookies

Required for the website to function properly, including authentication and security features.

Duration: Session or up to 30 days
Purpose: Website functionality and security

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information.

Duration: Up to 2 years
Purpose: Website analytics and improvements

Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences and settings to improve your experience.

Duration: Up to 1 year
Purpose: Personalization and preferences

Marketing Cookies

Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

Duration: Up to 1 year
Purpose: Advertising and marketing
