Cookie Policy

Learn how we use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on apiUI.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better browsing experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our site.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly, including authentication and security features.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings to improve your experience.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services.

Purpose: Authentication, security, and basic website functionality
Duration: Session or up to 30 days
Provider: apiUI

Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us know which pages are the most and least popular.

Purpose: Website analytics and performance monitoring
Duration: Up to 2 years
Provider: Google Analytics, apiUI

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

Purpose: Remember your preferences and settings
Duration: Up to 1 year
Provider: apiUI

Third-Party Cookies

We may also use third-party services that set their own cookies. These include:

  • Supabase: For authentication and database services
  • Google Analytics: For website analytics and performance tracking
  • Firebase: For hosting and additional analytics

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie rights by setting your preferences in the cookie consent banner that appears when you first visit our site.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from particular sites
  • Block all cookies from being placed
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page.
